# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Moroccan accounting firms, finance teams, and growing SMEs who need a clear, credible way to monitor unpaid invoices and decide what follow-up is needed next.

## Product Purpose

Reloncia makes receivables visible, prioritised, documented, and easier to act on. The public site should turn LinkedIn interest into qualified demo conversations through a tangible product preview.

## Brand Personality

Composed, exacting, and human. Reloncia should make financial operations feel calmer and more deliberate without performing corporate banking or startup hype.

## Anti-references

Avoid cryptocurrency aesthetics, generic blue SaaS, speculative AI claims, fake customer proof, dense banking dashboards, and all-dark interfaces.

## Design Principles

- Show the operational result before explaining features.
- Make the financial context feel composed, not intimidating.
- Prefer evidence and clarity to marketing superlatives.
- Use the product preview as the central proof of value.
- Keep every conversion step direct and low-friction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast expectations, preserve keyboard navigation and visible focus states, support reduced motion, and use French and English public copy.
