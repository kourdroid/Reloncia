# eFacturation

## Accounts Receivable Recovery for Moroccan Accounting Firms and SMEs

eFacturation is a Morocco-first platform that helps accounting firms and SMEs monitor, prioritize, and recover unpaid invoices before cash-flow pressure becomes a legal, operational, or client relationship problem.

This project is not a simple invoice editor. Its purpose is to answer one business question every day:

**Which unpaid invoices need action now, who is responsible, what has already been done, and what money can realistically be recovered faster?**

---

## The Problem It Solves

Many accounting firms and SMEs still manage unpaid invoices through scattered Excel files, email threads, WhatsApp messages, and manual follow-up lists. This creates several business problems:

- Finance teams do not have one reliable view of unpaid exposure.
- Accountants waste time checking multiple client files manually.
- Late-paying clients are discovered too late.
- Follow-up history is not consistently documented.
- Teams cannot prove when reminders were sent, through which channel, or with what result.
- Payment-delay risk is hard to monitor before invoices become critical.
- Monthly reporting to client companies is slow, repetitive, and often incomplete.

The result is slower cash collection, weaker client service, higher operational cost, and avoidable legal or compliance risk.

eFacturation centralizes the recovery workflow so accountants and company teams can move from passive tracking to active collection management.

## What The Platform Does

### 1. Cross-Company Risk Dashboard

Cabinet users can view unpaid invoice risk across all companies they are allowed to manage.

The dashboard highlights:

- Total unpaid amount in MAD.
- Number of invoices overdue by more than 30 days.
- Invoices approaching or exceeding legal-risk thresholds.
- Top late-paying clients.
- Companies requiring urgent follow-up.

This turns scattered invoice files into a daily action board.

### 2. Company Aging View

Each company has an aging view that groups unpaid invoices by delay bucket:

- 0-30 days.
- 31-60 days.
- 60+ days.

This helps teams prioritize collections based on urgency instead of reviewing invoices one by one.

### 3. Reminder Sending and Follow-Up History

Users can send reminders for unpaid invoices and keep a full history of follow-up actions.

Each reminder records:

- Date and time.
- Channel used.
- Recipient.
- Invoice link.
- Delivery or outcome status.
- User responsible for the action.

This creates a reliable operational record and reduces disputes around whether follow-up happened.

### 4. Excel Import and Cleanup

The platform is designed for the reality of accounting work: invoice data often starts in inconsistent Excel files.

eFacturation supports a review-and-confirm import workflow:

- Upload invoice files.
- Match inconsistent column names.
- Detect missing fields.
- Flag invalid amounts or dates.
- Detect duplicate invoice numbers.
- Link invoices to existing clients where possible.
- Confirm only validated data.

This reduces manual re-entry and improves the quality of the receivables database.

### 5. Law 69-21 Payment-Delay Risk Awareness

The platform flags invoices approaching the 60-day payment-delay threshold so users can act before the situation becomes critical.

This is especially important for Moroccan companies and accounting firms that need better visibility around payment-delay exposure.

The platform does not replace legal advice. It gives operational visibility so the business can act earlier and document follow-up clearly.

### 6. Monthly Company Reports

Accounting firms can generate monthly reports per company, including:

- Total unpaid amount.
- Aging breakdown.
- Reminder history.
- Top risk clients.
- Legal-risk invoices.

These reports create a recurring service artifact that cabinets can share with client companies.

## ROI: Where The Return Comes From

The return on investment comes from four main areas.

### 1. Faster Cash Recovery

The biggest ROI driver is collecting unpaid invoices earlier.

If a company has 1,000,000 MAD in unpaid invoices and the platform helps recover only 5% faster, that is 50,000 MAD of cash made available earlier.

This does not require creating new sales. It unlocks money the business has already earned.

### 2. Reduced Manual Work

Manual receivables tracking often requires accountants to:

- Open multiple Excel files.
- Reconcile invoice lists.
- Check due dates manually.
- Search emails and WhatsApp messages.
- Rebuild monthly reports.
- Re-enter imported invoice data.

If one accountant saves 5 hours per week and the internal cost is 150 MAD per hour, the operational saving is:

**5 hours x 150 MAD x 4 weeks = 3,000 MAD per month**

For a cabinet with multiple accountants, the savings scale quickly.

### 3. Better Client Retention for Cabinets

Accounting firms can use eFacturation as a value-added service, not only an internal tool.

Instead of telling clients "you have unpaid invoices," the cabinet can provide:

- A clear receivables dashboard.
- A monthly report.
- A documented reminder history.
- A list of clients creating payment risk.
- Early warnings before payment delays become critical.

This makes the cabinet more strategic and harder to replace.

### 4. Lower Risk and Better Documentation

Documented follow-up history reduces operational ambiguity.

The business can show:

- Which invoice was followed up.
- When the reminder was sent.
- Who sent it.
- Which channel was used.
- What the result was.

This improves internal accountability and supports stronger client discussions.

## What Makes It Different

### Built Around Recovery, Not Invoice Creation

Many tools focus on creating invoices. eFacturation focuses on recovering unpaid invoices after they already exist.

That makes it useful for cabinets and SMEs that already have invoice data but lack a structured recovery workflow.

### Designed For Accounting Firms Managing Multiple Companies

The platform supports cabinet-level visibility while keeping each company's data scoped and separated.

This matters because accounting firms need to manage many client companies without mixing access, reports, or financial records.

### Morocco-First Payment-Delay Awareness

The platform includes payment-delay risk visibility around the 60-day threshold, which is directly relevant to Moroccan business operations.

### Excel-Friendly Onboarding

The product accepts that real invoice data often starts in imperfect spreadsheets. It is built to help users clean, validate, and centralize that data.

### Follow-Up History As A Business Asset

Every reminder and recovery action becomes part of the invoice history. That history is useful for operations, reporting, management review, and client communication.
